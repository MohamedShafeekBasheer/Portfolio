New to SharePoint? The first question you get when you create a site is the one most people guess at: team site or communication site?

One question settles it: will most people work in this site, or read it?

Work together → team site. It comes with a Microsoft 365 group (so Teams, a shared calendar, Planner), and members can edit.

A few people publish for everyone else → communication site. No group, mostly read-only, built for pages and news. Think intranet home, IT help, HR policies.

A good pattern is to use both: HR drafts in a team site and publishes the finished policies on a communication site.

And choose the site address carefully. It's hard to change once people bookmark it.
