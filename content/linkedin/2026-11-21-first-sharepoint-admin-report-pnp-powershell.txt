New to PnP PowerShell? This is the script I'd start with.

It answers a question every SharePoint team gets eventually: how many sites do we have, who can share them externally, and how much space are they using?

One connection, one command, one CSV:

Connect-PnPOnline -Url https://contoso-admin.sharepoint.com -ClientId <your-app-id> -Interactive
Get-PnPTenantSite | Select-Object Url, Template, SharingCapability, StorageUsageCurrent | Export-Csv .\sites.csv -NoTypeInformation

Two things that catch beginners out:

1. Older samples sign in with -UseWebLogin, or -Interactive without a ClientId. That no longer works. You need your own Entra ID app registration first.
2. Use PowerShell 7. Current PnP PowerShell won't run in Windows PowerShell 5.1.

Based on Kasper Larsen's sample in the PnP Script Samples library, which has 400+ community scripts worth exploring.

Full walkthrough, plus a one-line filter for externally shared sites: https://mohamedshafeekbasheer.github.io/Portfolio/notes/first-sharepoint-admin-report-pnp-powershell/
