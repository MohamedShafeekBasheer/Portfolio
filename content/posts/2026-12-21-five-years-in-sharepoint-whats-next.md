I've spent five years in SharePoint, Microsoft 365 and the Power Platform. This note is a short summary of what I do, for anyone who found me through these notes, and what I'm looking for next.

## What I work on

- **New business systems on Microsoft 365.** SPFx (React, TypeScript), Power Apps and Power Automate, connected to Azure Functions, Azure SQL and Dynamics 365 when needed. For example, a [contract platform](/work/contract-management-platform/) that took a process from days to minutes.
- **Legacy rescues.** InfoPath retirement, broken SharePoint Designer workflows, ageing on-premises farms. For example, [8 InfoPath forms for 500+ users](/work/infopath-to-spfx-modernisation/).
- **Migrations.** Google Workspace to Microsoft 365, tenant to tenant, and restructuring. For example, [45,000 documents with zero loss](/work/group-document-migration/).
- **Security and governance.** Permission audits, external sharing, Conditional Access, DLP and incident response.

Right now I'm the only person running SharePoint, Microsoft 365 and the Power Platform for a five-company group in the GCC. Before that I worked at a Big Four global delivery centre and an IT services firm, for clients in the US, UK, Canada and the GCC.

## What I'm looking for

I'm open to conversations about:

- **SharePoint Developer**
- **Microsoft 365 Consultant**
- **Power Platform Developer**
- **Microsoft 365 / SharePoint Architect**

in **the GCC and Middle East, Singapore or India**, or remote. I'm based in the GCC, with a transferable residence visa.

I also take on focused consulting work: SharePoint reviews, governance and remediation, and migrations.
