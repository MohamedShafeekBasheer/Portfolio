About

Hi, I'm Shafeek.

I started in December 2021 as a trainee at Avasoft in Chennai, building React and Node apps and helping on SharePoint migrations. Within a few months I was on migration projects full time, and over the next two and a half years I worked on six client programmes — the biggest was a 5,000-user move from Google Workspace.

After a short stint building SPFx web parts at Colan InfoTech, I joined EY Global Delivery Services, where I spent most of a year on internal risk, leave and resourcing apps. That's where I learned to ship inside a strict change-management process without slowing down.

Since October 2025 I've been an IT Analyst at Al Qahtani Beverages in Abha, Saudi Arabia. I look after SharePoint and Microsoft 365 for 500+ people, I'm retiring the company's InfoPath forms, and I'm building an internal assistant on Azure OpenAI.

Outside the day job I take consulting work and mentor people who are starting out on the Microsoft stack. I want to keep working with teams in different countries — and I'm ready to move, with my family, for the right role.
