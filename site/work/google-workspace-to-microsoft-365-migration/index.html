Moving 5,000 people from Google Workspace to Microsoft 365

Drive, Shared Drives, Sites, Gmail and Groups — the whole Google workplace moved to Microsoft 365 inside six months, with a tracking app so everyone could see where each workload stood.

Client
Enterprise client, via Avasoft
Where

Result

The situation

A large enterprise wanted off Google Workspace entirely: Drive, Shared Drives, Google Sites, Gmail and Groups. The deadline was six months and the business couldn't stop working while it happened.

What I did

  • Google Drive and Shared Drives to SharePoint with ShareGate and Migration Manager.
  • Google Sites to SharePoint, classic and modern.
  • Gmail to Exchange Online / Outlook.
  • Google Groups to Microsoft 365 Groups and Teams.
  • Built a migration tracking app so the team could follow every workload in real time instead of chasing spreadsheets.

What changed

  • 5,000+ users migrated within the six-month window.
  • Cutover completed with zero data loss.
  • Replaced the client's legacy Google Sheets workflows with a governed SharePoint application after go-live.

  • ShareGate
