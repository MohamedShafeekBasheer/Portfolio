Closing an external sharing leak in SharePoint Online

A sharing misconfiguration had exposed internal documents to users at a partner company. I audited the tenant, revoked every unauthorised link and guest, and rebuilt the sharing policy so it couldn't happen again.

Client
Confidential organisation
Where
Canada
Length
2 weeks
Result
All unauthorised access removed within 48 hours

The situation

An external sharing setting in SharePoint Online had been left too open. Sensitive internal documents were reachable by users at a Canadian partner organisation, and the exposure ran across several site collections.

Nobody knew exactly how far it went. The first job was to find out — quickly — and stop it.

What I did

  • Audited the whole tenant with PowerShell and the Microsoft 365 admin center.
  • Listed every externally shared link, guest account and anonymous access link.
  • Revoked access in a controlled order so business-critical sharing wasn't broken by accident.
  • Reset sharing policy at both tenant and site-collection level.
  • Added monitoring alerts and Conditional Access rules to catch drift early.

What changed

  • 100% of unauthorised external access removed within 48 hours.
  • Sharing policy tightened and enforced with Conditional Access.
  • Incident report delivered to stakeholders.
  • No recurrence since the fix.

Tools

  • SharePoint Online
  • PowerShell
  • Microsoft Entra ID
  • M365 admin center
  • Conditional Access
