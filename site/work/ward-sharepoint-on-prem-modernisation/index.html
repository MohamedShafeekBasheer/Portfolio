Stabilising 14 SharePoint on-prem apps for a US logistics company

Expense, accident-report and approval apps on an ageing SharePoint farm were creating duplicate records every day. I found the race conditions, fixed 50+ broken workflows and rebuilt the Accident Report app from scratch.

Client
WARD
Where
USA
Length
8 months
Result
14+ apps stabilised, duplicate submissions gone

The situation

WARD ran 14+ business-critical applications — expenses, accident reports, approvals — on an ageing SharePoint on-premises environment.

Forms were creating duplicate entries, the sequential ID generators kept breaking, and an unstable Node setup made every fix risky. Staff felt it every day.

What I did

  • Traced the duplicates to front-end race conditions when two people submitted at the same time, and fixed them.
  • Upgraded the Node versions the apps depended on.
  • Rebuilt the Accident Report application end-to-end with a new UI and a reliable back end.
  • Fixed 50+ SharePoint Designer workflows across the portfolio.

What changed

  • All 14+ applications stable.
  • Zero duplicate submissions after the fix.
  • New Accident Report application live in production.
